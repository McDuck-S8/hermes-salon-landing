## 1. MadBid.com — Best for Crypto-Native International Sellers

### Platform Overview
- **URL:** https://madbid.com
- **Telegram Category:** https://madbid.com/telegram
- **Founded:** 2008 (rebranded 2020s as social media marketplace)
- **Type:** Escrow marketplace with guarantor service

### Fees & Commissions
| Transaction Size | Commission | Minimum Fee |
|-----------------|------------|-------------|
| Up to $250 | 3.5% | $10 |
| Over $250 | 4% | $10 |

### Payment Methods
- **Crypto:** USDT (TRC20, ERC20, TON), BTC, ETH
- **Fiat:** PayPal, Bank Card (via payment processors)
- **Escrow:** Built-in guarantor holds funds until transfer complete

### Verification Process
1. **Seller verification:** Telegram account ownership proof (admin rights screenshot, bot token verification)
2. **Channel verification:** Subscriber count, engagement stats, niche confirmation
3. **Guarantor holds:** Buyer pays → Guarantor confirms → Seller transfers channel ownership → Buyer confirms → Funds released

### How to List
1. Register at madbid.com
2. Go to "Sell" → Select "Telegram Channel"
3. Fill listing: niche, subscribers, engagement, price, screenshots
4. Set price in USD (auto-converts to USDT)
5. Wait for moderation (usually <24h)
6. Guarantor contacts you when buyer found

### Pros / Cons

## ⚠️ Critical Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Platform Exposure | Mitigation |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Telegram bans transferred channels** | ALL platforms | Sell channels with original phone/email; avoid "flipped" accounts; disclose transfer history |
| **Fake stats / bot subscribers** | PlayerUp, P2P groups | Require @ChannelAnalyticsBot or Telemetr live stats; screen-record stats |
| **Chargeback / payment reversal** | PayPal, Card payments | **Only accept crypto (USDT/BTC/TON)** — irreversible |
| **Middleman exit scam** | PlayerUp, P2P | Use only TOP-RATED middlemen with 50+ vouches; never new accounts |
| **Platform shutdown / seizure** | MadBid, PlayerUp | Withdraw funds immediately after sale; don't store balance |
| **KYC / sanctions (RU platforms)** | Telderi | Use crypto payouts; non-RU sellers may face withdrawal delays |
