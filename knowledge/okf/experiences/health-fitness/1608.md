Supplement Inventory Arbitrage (Dropshipping Overlay) (strategy): Monitor iHerb/Amazon pricing for 100 best-selling supplements. Identify price drops >15% and auto-purchase discounted inventory. Resell on eBay/Walmart marketplace at MSRP. Arbitrage: spread of 15-30% per unit – each transaction $5-$15 profit. Use inventory management API to auto-list and ship via Amazon FBA (merchant fulfilled).
