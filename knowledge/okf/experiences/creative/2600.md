FIX: pattern_detector.py _find_missing_knowledge() had 3 bugs causing 4950 false patterns (should be 10). Bug 1: break on line 184 only exited inner loop, not outer - generated N-1 patterns per department instead of 1. Bug 2: O(n2) pairwise comparison of 3055 signals = 4.7M pairs, every pair with 3+ common words = new pattern. Bug 3: no deduplication - same signal pairs appeared in dozens of patterns. Fix: replaced with TF-IDF cosine similarity (threshold 0.5), MAX 3 patterns per dept, MAX 10 total, deduplication via seen_pairs set. Result: 4950 -> 10 patterns, 0.2s instead of O(n2). Lesson: break in nested loops only exits innermost loop - use flag or function return. Word overlap is too crude for similarity - use TF-IDF weighting.
