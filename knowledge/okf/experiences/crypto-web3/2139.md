Crypto/Web3 Arbitrage & Income - DeFi, Staking, MEV, Working Without Banks

1. DeFi Primitives: AMMs - Uniswap V2 for stable pairs (USDC-USDT), V3 for concentrated liquidity (±5-10% range for max fees, requires frequent rebalancing). Curve for stablecoins + CRV rewards. Impermanent loss formula: `2 * sqrt(P2/P1) / (1 + P2/P1) - 1`. 50% price move = ~5.7% IL, needs >$10M pool volume for fees to cover. Yield farming: track APR in project tokens, not stablecoins - token depreciation often makes real returns negative.

2. Staking: Native ETH (32 ETH, ~3.5-4% APY, requires node). Liquid staking: stETH (Lido) / rETH (Rocket Pool) ~3-3.5% APY, usable in DeFi (Aave collateral). Restaking: EigenLayer + AVS for additional 6-8% APR, but higher slashing risk. Max 20% of ETH portfolio in restaking. Monitor protocol updates.

3. MEV Arbitrage: Professional bot territory, but accessible via Flashbots Auction RPC or custom searcher (Python + mev-inspect). Atomic DEX-CEX arb requires flash loans (Aave 0% for 1 block) - gas eats profit if spread <0.3%. Always simulate in Tenderly first. Sandwich attacks dead due to Flashbots Protect private mempools. Focus: backrunning (arb after large swaps).

4. Cross-Chain Arb: Price diffs 0.5-1% between L2s, but bridge fees $1-5 + 3-30 min finality. Only viable with stablecoins (USDC.e Arbitrum vs USDC Optimism). LayerZero for native cross-chain swaps. Use canonical assets (native USDC) NOT wrapped (wUSDC). Best spread: Base vs Polygon (different pool depths). Base has lower gas = advantage.

5. Stablecoin Strategies: Best risk-adjusted: Morpho (8-12% USDC via Aave markets), Convex stable pools (up to 15% with CVX tokens, but token inflation reduces real APR). Delta-neutral: borrow USDC on Aave → deposit Curve → short farm token on perp DEX (Hyperliquid) = 20-30% APY, rebalance every 2-3 days. Funding rate arb: spot long + perp short when funding >0.1%/8hr = 15-25% APY no price risk.

6. Risk Management: Max 5% capital in new protocol without Trail of Bits/CertiK audit. Insurance: Nexus Mutual (~2.5%/yr coverage) covers smart contract bugs, NOT market risk. Portfolio: 40% stables (Morpho/Curve), 30% ETH staking, 20% cross-chain arb, 10% high-yield farming (weekly review).

7. Tools/Infra: Flashbots private RPC (avoid sandwich) - configure in MetaMask/Hardhat. Testing: Foundry (faster than Hardhat) with mainnet fork for gas/liquidity checks. Simulation: Tenderly for multi-step debug. Data: DefiLlama for TVL/yields. RPC: Alchemy/QuickNode ($49/mo, <100ms latency critical for bots).

P2P: Only Binance/Bybit/HTX verified merchants, avoid >1M RUB/tx (115-FZ risk), split 300-500k RUB across days. Cashout: T-Bank friendly, Raiffeisen cautious, Sber only if IP. On-ramp: BestChange P2P exchangers, always check counterparty reputation.
