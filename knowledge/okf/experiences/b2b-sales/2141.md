B2B Sales - Cold Email, Cold Calling, Objection Handling, Closing Deals

1. Cold Email Structure: Subject line = 80% success. Personalized trigger ("Idea for [Company] + [Competitor]" or "Question about [New Product]"), avoid "newsletter"/"partnership". Personalization = specific pain ("Noticed your sales team spends 20 hrs/week on manual data entry"), not just name. Value prop: 1 sentence ("We cut cost-per-lead 40% for similar co in 3 weeks"). CTA = low friction ("2 questions Wednesday?"). Sequence: email → 2-day bump → social proof day 4 → break-up day 7 → final day 10 with unsubscribe.

2. Lead Gen & Prospecting: ICP = 4 axes: industry + revenue + tech stack + current pains (NOT titles). Apollo/ZoomInfo 70-80% accuracy, verify via Hunter + LinkedIn Sales Nav cross-check. Decision-maker or budget influencer only. Monthly list hygiene via NeverBounce/ZeroBounce - bounce rate >2% kills domain reputation.

3. Discovery Calls: SPIN framework: Situation (current process), Problem (what's broken), Implication (cost of inaction), Need-payoff (value of fix). MEDDIC: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process. Uncover consequences of inaction ("If nothing changes, lose 15% market share in 6 months"). Record prospect's selection criteria (price, timeline, support) to address on demo.

4. Demo/Presentation: Start with prospect's pain visualization, NOT "About Us" slide. Max 3 features in 20 min. ROI narrative: "Managers spend 10 hrs/week on reports → we cut to 2 hrs → savings 8 hrs × $50/hr × 12 = $4,800/yr". On "send info": "I'll send it, but let's look at your actual deal in the system so materials match your reality" → interactive.

5. Proposal & Negotiation: Price anchoring - show max tier first, then optimal (most popular, highest margin), then base. 3 tiers: basic, optimal, VIP. Contract: clear implementation phases, SLA, penalty clauses for missed metrics. Champion must introduce Economic Buyer - agree who signs, get champion to schedule meeting BEFORE sending proposal.

6. Objection Handling: "Expensive" → Feel-Felt-Found: "Understand, others felt same, but they found ROI in 3 months". "No budget" → "What would you buy if budget existed?" → strip modules to minimum viable check. LAER: Listen, Acknowledge, Explore ("why does this concern you?"), Respond with facts. Price, timing, competitor, authority, need frameworks.

7. Closing: Assumptive close ("Start Monday or Tuesday?" not "Buy?"). Trial close ("If integration solved, sign tomorrow?") reveals hidden fears. Champion activation: give them internal email template with ROI numbers + competitor comparison. Procurement: prep full doc package (technical spec, licenses, 10 FAQ answers) → sign in 2 days not 2 weeks.

8. Tools/CRM: Startup = HubSpot (free 5 users) or Pipedrive. Enterprise = Salesforce (needs admin). Outreach/Salesloft for sequences (Tue-Wed 9-11am best). Gong/Chorus/Chorus for call recording + analysis - find where interest drops, feed back to scripts. Apollo/LinkedIn Sales Nav for data.
