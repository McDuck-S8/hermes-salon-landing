AI Content Factory - Local Neural Networks for Photo/Video Generation Without Subscriptions

1. Hardware Requirements: SDXL needs 8-12GB VRAM (RTX 3070/4060 Ti), +ControlNet/LoRA = 12GB. Flux.1-dev FP16 = 24GB (RTX 4090), FP8/NF4 quantization = 12-16GB. Video models: 16-24GB+. RAM: 32GB minimum. Storage: 1TB NVMe (models 5-20GB each, LoRA training cache 50-100GB).

2. Stable Diffusion Ecosystem: Automatic1111 for quick prototyping. ComfyUI for production - 2-3x faster, node-based, reusable JSON workflows. Model format: .safetensors (secure) > .ckpt. GGUF (4-5 bit) for VRAM savings. LoRA training: Kohya_ss, 1024x1024, 2-4 batch, 100-200 images, 30-60 min on 4090.

3. Flux Ecosystem: Flux.1-schnell (4 steps) > SDXL quality in 1-2 sec on 4090. Flux.1-dev for control + ControlNet (Depth/Canny/Pose, ~5GB each). FP8/NF4 quantization mandatory for 12GB cards - <5% quality loss, 1.5x speed. ComfyUI workflows more complex (T5 text encoder nodes) - use Civitai templates.

4. Video Generation: SVD (14-25 frames, 5-10 min on 16GB). AnimateDiff for character animation (higher quality, complex setup). LTX Video fastest for short clips (1-2 sec, 768x512, ~10GB). Mochi photorealistic up to 5 sec, needs 24GB even quantized. Always use --xformers + --flash-attention for 30% VRAM reduction. Preload latents for batch frames.

5. Local Inference Optimization: CPU offloading (ModelPatcher in ComfyUI) for T5/CLIP encoders. xformers + Flash Attention = 20-40% speedup, 15-20% VRAM reduction. Batch 8-16 images: min steps (20-30), disable intermediate saves = 2x throughput. Quantization: GGUF/GPTQ/AWQ for all models.

6. Pipeline Automation: ComfyUI headless mode (--headless) + REST API (/prompt) from Python scripts. Save workflows as JSON templates with variables (prompt, seed, steps). APScheduler for cron: nightly batch of 500 images from keyword list. Integrate with Telegram bots, backends.

7. Quality Control: LAION Aesthetic Predictor (1-10 score), reject <6 (blurry/distorted). NSFW filter: nsfw_detector (TF) or CLIP blacklist tokens. Auto-select best: CLIP-score (prompt-image embedding similarity), take top 10%.

8. Business Models: Adobe Stock/Shutterstock accept AI if marked - upload 50-100/week thematic series (interiors, food, people 4K) = $0.5-2/month passive per image. Business packages: 10 logos or 5 social videos = $300+. AI influencer: 3-5 posts/day consistent face (Flux+LoRA) → OnlyFans/Patreon (disclose AI). Print-on-demand: Redbubble/Teespring patterns/covers = 20% commission, zero inventory cost.
