AI Agent Frameworks Comparison (Digibase Media / Jeff): AutoGen vs CrewAI vs LangGraph vs OpenAI Swarm. LangGraph: best for production, stateful graphs, human-in-the-loop. CrewAI: easiest for beginners, role-based agents. AutoGen: most flexible, multi-agent chat. Swarm: lightweight, educational. For production CPA/arbitrage: LangGraph (state management, checkpointing). For rapid prototyping: CrewAI.
