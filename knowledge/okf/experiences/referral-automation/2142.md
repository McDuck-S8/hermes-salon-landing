Referral Automation - Earning from Recommendations Without Creating Your Own Product

1. Major Referral Programs: Travelpayouts (travel - up to 40% commission, $10-50/booking, 30-day cookie). Admitad (multi-vertical RU). SaaS: Webflow ($105/paid user), Airtable (up to $200/business account), Notion/Dropbox/Miro/Linear/Framer. Crypto: Binance (40% trading fees), Bybit (50% fees). Hosting: Hostinger ($100/new client). Education: Stepik (30% course sales), Udemy/Coursera.

2. Commission Structures: Always choose recurring (lifetime) for subscription products - Miro 20% LTV = $500+/user. Cookie windows: Travelpayouts/SaaS 30-90 days, crypto 7 days (need fast-decision channels like YouTube). Attribution: default last-click, use multi-touch via UTM for complex funnels.

3. Packaging Ready Solutions: Don't just share links - give referrals turnkey systems. Build Telegram bot showing live discounts + comparisons (2 hrs Python/aiogram). Landing page with savings calculator ("How much save on Hostinger/year") capturing email for nurture. Embed your referral ID everywhere. 3-5x conversion increase vs naked links.

4. Acquisition Funnels: Telegram channel with case studies ("How I made $200 on Binance referrals/week", "SaaS affiliate comparison"). YouTube demos with personal experience + link in description (2-5% click conversion vs 0.5% posts). Email sequence: #1 case with numbers, #2 product features, #3 "What's stopping you?" to convert cold subs.

5. Automation: UTM per channel (utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=hostinger_dec). Tapfiliate/Rewardful for postback integration (server-side conversion tracking real-time). Auto-payouts via crypto/PayPal for referral loyalty. Google Looker Studio dashboard with UTM + metrics (clicks, conversions, LTV) to identify & scale winners.

6. Viral Loops & K-Factor: K-factor >1 needed. Double-sided rewards: both get $10, milestone bonus $50 at 5 referrals. Crypto exchanges: Bybit gives $30 new + $15 referrer = mass referrals via groups. Promote via Telegram contests ("Most referrals/month = iPad") + anonymous leaderboard.

7. Compliance & Legal: Russia: self-employed 4% (B2C) or 6% (B2B), IP USN 6%. Track payouts per platform, pay tax by 25th next month. International: check if royalty vs service fee, may need foreign currency account. Mandatory disclosure: "referral link" or #ad per FTC/Roskomnadzor, or risk account ban for hidden ads.

8. Tools: Rewardful (Stripe/Chargebee integration, auto-commission for SaaS). Tapfiliate (flexible rules for crypto/courses/hosting). Zero budget: Google Sheets + UTM + Posthog (free 1M events). ReferralCandy for e-commerce. FirstPromoter for B2B long sales cycles. Choose by primary offer type.
