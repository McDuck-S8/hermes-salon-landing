# Salon Bot Client Acquisition — Full Workflow

Source: Session 2026-05-27, from "Нейропросвещение" channel insights + salon bot project

## Vibe Coding Approach

1. **Build template bot** (1-2 hours with AI) — booking flow, master calendar, admin panel
2. **Record video walkthrough** — show the bot working in Telegram, explain features
3. **Publish on YouTube/Telegram** — even 100 views = potential client

## Finding Real Salon Clients

### Method 1: Telegram Group Monitoring (Userbot)
1. Search Telegram for salon/beauty groups in target city (e.g. "Симферополь красота", "Крым салоны")
2. Join groups using Pyrogram userbot (see `telegram-service-bot` → `references/telegram-userbot-setup.md`)
3. Monitor for salon owners posting about business problems (scheduling, no-shows, client management)
4. Send personalized DM: "Привет! Видел ваш вопрос про запись клиентов. У меня есть готовое решение — вот демо: [link]"

### Method 2: Video-Based Outreach
1. Find salon owners on YouTube/Instagram who post content
2. Watch their content, understand their business
3. Send message: "Посмотрел ваш контент. Заметил что [конкретная проблема]. Вот демо бота который решает это: [video link]"

### Method 3: Direct Search
1. 2GIS/Google Maps → list of salons in target city
2. Check which ones have Telegram presence (bot, channel, group)
3. Salons WITHOUT Telegram bot = prospects
4. Contact via phone/Instagram with offer

