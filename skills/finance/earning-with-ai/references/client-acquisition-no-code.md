# Selling No-Code Projects — Key Insights

Source: YouTube video on selling no-code/AI projects to businesses (May 2026)

## Core Principle
**Client buys RESULT, not code.** They don't care if it's Python, Next.js, Claude, or Codex. They want a solution that works and pays for itself.

## Three Product Categories

| Category | Price Range | Time | Example |
|----------|-------------|------|---------|
| Telegram bots | 15-50K₽ | 2-5 days | Task automation, booking, support |
| Mini-apps for Telegram | 50-100K₽ | 1-2 weeks | Closed club, CRM interface |
| Full applications (CRM, dashboards) | 200K-500K₽+ | 20+ days | Fulfillment system, client portal |

## How to Find Clients

1. **Telegram chats** — go where your niche hangs out (seller communities, business chats)
2. **YouTube** — even 100 views = 1 client. Long video builds trust better than a post
3. **Direct messages WITH FREE ANALYSIS** — study the business first, send a mini-audit
4. **Partnerships** — designers → developers → clients chain

## Message Formula (DO NOT send generic proposals)

❌ "Привет, нужен бот? Я умею делать ботов."
✅ "Привет. Изучил ваш бизнес. Вот что заметил: [бесплатный разбор с конкретными цифрами]. Могу решить эту проблему за X рублей."

**Key**: Preparation > pitch. Spend 30min studying the prospect before writing.

## 30-Day Launch Timeline

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Define niche + offer |
| 2 | Build demo project (sells better than any proposal) |
| 3 | 30-50 outreach messages/day. 200 messages = 1 client guaranteed |
| 4 | First case study in portfolio |

## Client Acquisition Math
- 50 outreach/day × 4 days = 200 messages
- 200 messages → 1-2 clients (if not spamming)
- First 10 projects at 10K₽ = practice, not income
- After 10 projects → raise prices, niche portfolio forms

## Key Quotes
- "Клиенту плевать на процесс. Ему нужен результат."
- "Демо-проект продаёт лучше, чем коммерческое предложение."
- "Эксперт — это когда за твою способность готовы платить больше, чем другому."
- "Продавать не 'я напишу бота', а 'я увеличу ваши бронирования на 30%'."
