# Telegram CPA & Affiliate Monetization Research (2026-06-25)

## Telegram Market 2026
- 1B+ active accounts, 500M daily, 2.5M new daily
- Low ad saturation — feels new, rare mix of reach + low fatigue
- No ID verification — fast testing, bold creatives allowed
- CIS + India lead, Europe/LATAM/Africa emerging
- Window closing fast — early phase NOW, not next year

## Three Scalable Models

### 1. Telegram Funnels (replace landing pages)
- Traffic from Meta/YouTube/push/SEO → bot → channel → conversion
- Bot = email + retargeting + CRM combined
- Bot introduces offer → pushes CTA → triggers upsell on conversion
- Automated nudges when user hesitates

### 2. Channel Placements (media buying)
- Buy posts in niche channels
- Tools: Telemetr, TelegaStat for channel analytics
- Compare subscribers vs avg post views for real activity
- Negotiate directly with admins
- Agencies handle end-to-end for 10-15% fee

### 3. Telegram Ads (official platform)
- CPM bidding, GEO + channel-interest targeting
- Per-user frequency capping (built-in retargeting)
- Flexible daily spend
- Analytics: CTR, CPC, CPM, CPS, joins, views
- CIS: can route traffic to offer pages directly
- Still underused and underpriced

## Telegram Native Affiliate Program
- Earn Stars from mini app referrals
- Free to join — Settings > My Stars
- Generate referral link, connect to Star balance
- Commission on all Star purchases by referred users
- Can link personal balance or channel/app balance

## Working Model: Tara Bot (57★, MIT)
- GitHub: thaolst/tara-bot
- Features: flight search (Google Flights), price comparison (Google Shopping), affiliate links
- Daily price monitoring (9AM)
- Claude AI for natural conversation
- Prompt caching (90% token savings)

- Health check endpoint

## Creative Angles That Work
- Benefit promise (lead with concrete payoff)
- Curiosity hook (open a loop reader must close)
- Free trial or instant win (lower barrier)
- Scarcity/urgency (give reason to act now)

## Key Insight
Telegram = distribution layer, NOT the business.
Build paywall + payments where YOU control.
Bot collects leads → monetize via CPA → retain via retainer.
