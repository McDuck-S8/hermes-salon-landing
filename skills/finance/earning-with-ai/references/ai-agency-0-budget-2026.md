## What It Is

1. **Workflow Automation** — CRM ↔ email ↔ calendar (n8n, Make.com)
2. **Conversational AI** — chatbots, phone agents, booking (Botpress, Flowise)

4. **Lead Generation & Sales** — auto-qualification, follow-up
5. **Predictive Analytics** — dashboards, forecasting
