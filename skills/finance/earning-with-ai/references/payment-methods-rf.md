# Payment Methods for RF/Crimea

Detailed guide for receiving payments in Russia and Crimea (2025).

## Russian Platforms (Direct to Card)

### FL.ru
- Commission: 10-20%
- Payment: Sber, Tinkoff, VTB, any RF card
- Timeline: 1-5 business days
- Pros: No crypto needed, direct RUB
- Cons: High commission

### Kwork
- Commission: 20%
- Payment: Any RF card
- Timeline: 1-3 business days
- Pros: Simple, fast
- Cons: High commission

### Habr Freelance
- Commission: 10-15%
- Payment: RF cards
- Timeline: 1-5 business days
- Pros: Tech-focused clients
- Cons: Smaller market

## E-Wallets

### ЮMoney (Yandex.Money)
- Register: yoomoney.ru
- Link: Any RF card
- Receive: Transfers from clients
- Withdraw to card: 1-3%
- Pros: Anonymity, convenient for freelance
- Cons: Need to verify for large amounts

### QIWI
- Register: qiwi.com
- Virtual card available
- Withdraw to card: 2-5%
- Pros: Fast, convenient
- Cons: Less popular than ЮMoney

## Crypto (For International)

### Exchanges for RF (No Sanctions)

**Bybit (Recommended)**
- URL: bybit.com
- KYC: No KYC up to 2 BTC/day
- P2P: RF cards (Sber, Tinkoff, VTB)
- Commission: 0% (P2P)
- Pros: Best for RF, fast, reliable
- Cons: Need VPN for main site

**OKX**
- URL: okx.com
- KYC: No KYC up to 10 BTC/day
- P2P: RF cards
- Commission: 0% (P2P)
- Pros: Good alternative to Bybit
- Cons: Less P2P liquidity

**Gate.io**
- URL: gate.io
- KYC: No KYC
- P2P: RF cards
- Commission: 0% (P2P)
- Pros: Many coins
- Cons: Less popular

**Huobi (HTX)**
- URL: htx.com
- KYC: No KYC
- P2P: RF cards
- Commission: 0% (P2P)
- Pros: Established
- Cons: Less RF-focused

### How to Receive Crypto

**Method 1: Direct Payment**
- Provide BTC/USDT address to client
- Client sends crypto directly
- Commission: 0%
- Pros: No middleman
- Cons: Client must have crypto

**Method 2: P2P on Exchange**
- Create buy order on Bybit/OKX
- Specify RF card (Sber/Tinkoff)
- Client sends RUB
- You receive USDT
- Commission: 0-1%
- Pros: Fast, convenient
- Cons: Need exchange account

**Method 3: Exchangers**
- Use bestchange.ru aggregator
- Select: USDT → Sberbank
- Choose exchanger with best rate
- Commission: 1-3%
- Pros: Fast, many options
- Cons: Check reputation

### How to Withdraw Crypto to RF Card

**Via P2P (Best Method):**
1. Register on Bybit/OKX
2. Go to P2P section
3. Select "Sell USDT"
4. Choose Sber/Tinkoff card
5. Receive RUB on card

**Via Exchangers:**
1. Go to bestchange.ru
2. Select: USDT → Sberbank
3. Choose exchanger
4. Transfer USDT
5. Receive RUB on card

**Commissions:**
- P2P: 0-1%
- Exchangers: 1-3%
- Bank fee: 0%

## Tax Options

### Self-Employed (Recommended)
- Register: npd.nalog.ru
- Tax: 4% (individuals), 6% (legal)
- Limit: 2.4M ₽/year
- Pros: Legal, simple
- Cons: Can't hire employees

### Individual Entrepreneur (IP)
- Tax: 6% (USN) or 15% (USN "income-expenses")
- Pros: Can hire, more opportunities
- Cons: More complex, reporting

### Without Registration
- Receive on card/crypto
- Pros: Maximum simplicity
- Cons: Illegal, risk of card blocking

**Recommendation:** Start as self-employed (4% tax), switch to IP when income grows.

## Recommended Setup

1. **Primary:** ЮMoney for Russian clients
2. **Secondary:** Bybit P2P for international
3. **Backup:** Bestchange.ru exchangers
4. **Tax:** Self-employed (npd.nalog.ru)
