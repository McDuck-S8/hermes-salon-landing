### Key Learnings
1. **50 sites > 5 sites** — need volume to see patterns, not just ideas
2. **Benchmark is mandatory** — can't price without knowing market
3. **Quality proof required** — raw HTML = embarrassing, not sellable
4. **Revenue is downstream** — maturity → capability → offering → revenue

### User's Core Philosophy
"Как студент который окончил вуз и вышел в жизнь"
- Student doesn't sell services before learning
- Student doesn't offer expertise before having it
- Student researches, practices, builds portfolio
- THEN enters the market with confidence

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
1. "I have a landing page" ≠ "I have a product"
2. "I know how to build X" ≠ "I can sell X"
3. "I researched for 5 minutes" ≠ "I benchmarked the market"
4. "I created 1 demo" ≠ "I have studio-quality proof"
