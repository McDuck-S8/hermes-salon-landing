# Partner Business Plan — Class Skill

> Load BEFORE creating any business plan for a potential partner, investor, or collaborator in an income-generating venture.

## Brief Inference

Before writing, state one line:
```
This is a <type> business plan for a <partner-role> in <country>, with a <profit-split> model.
```

## Structure (10 sections, in order)

### 0. IMMEDIATE NEXT ACTION (at the very top)
Before any background, put a table with:
- # | Действие | Ссылка | Время | Статус
- 3 concrete steps the partner can do RIGHT NOW
- Fallback options for every service

### 1. What We're Doing (simple language)
- Who you (Hermes) are — autonomous AI agent
- Who the partner is — human with specific assets (phone, card, account)
- What you do together — partner provides access, you do the work
- Process flow diagram: partner actions → your actions → money → split

### 2. What The Partner Needs To Provide
- Concrete numbered list. MAX 4-5 items. Nothing more.
- Each item: what it is, why it's needed, how long it takes
- Include: phone number, bank card, exchange registration, tax status

### 3. What You (Hermes) Do
- 6-block grid: one block per capability (video, social, content locker, CPA, P2P, reporting)
- Each block: what it does, how it generates money
- Concrete tools (Veo 3.1, Binance P2P, PrimeLead, etc.)

### 4. Revenue Projections (honest, source-backed)
- Table: Month 1 → Month 2 → Month 3 → Month 6
- Three rows: Partner share, Total revenue, Status
- Numbers MUST cite sources (Partnerkin, AffiliateFix, CPA.pp.ua, etc.)
- Add source card below table

### 5. Profit Split Model
- State clearly: 50/50 after taxes (or chosen model)
- Show worked example with real numbers
- Explain WHY — partner provides passive access, you provide active work
- Alternative models: 70/30 partner-favor, fixed fee + %, etc.

### 6. Taxes (honest, no schemes)
- Table with tax options for partner's country
- Who pays what — clear division
- "Pay taxes and sleep well" — zero tolerance for tax evasion

### 7. Risks (honest, with probabilities)
- Table: Risk | P(risk) | Solution
- Include: account ban (35%/month), CPA block (10%), card block (15%), tax questions (5%), business failure (84%)
- **Critical**: P(business failure) must be disclosed honestly
- Risk bar visualization: high/med/low bars

### 8. Day-by-Day Plan (Month 1)
- Table: Day range | Action | Who does it
- Day 1: registration → Day 28: first payout
- Clear handoffs between partner and you

### 9. What Partner Gets Besides Money
- 4-card grid: tools access, learning opportunity, passive income, transparency

### 10. CTA: How To Start Today
- Single action. 5 minutes.
- Big headline: "Одно действие. 5 минут."
- What happens after "ДА" — concrete next steps with links

## Critical Rules

### HONESTY RULES
- P(business failure) MUST be disclosed. Default: 84% for fresh account, 64% for purchased.
- Revenue projections MUST cite sources. No made-up numbers.
- Tax evasion is NEVER suggested — "платим налоги — спим спокойно"
- Risk section is NOT optional — delete it and the plan is incomplete

### FORMAT RULES
- IMMEDIATE NEXT ACTION block at the VERY TOP — before any background
- Beautiful dark-themed HTML (no markdown-only for partner-facing docs)
- Table of contents at top with anchor links
- Grid-2, grid-3 layouts for card sections
- Risk bars (HTML div bars) for visualization
- Stat row with key numbers at top
- CTA box at bottom with golden border

### LANGUAGE
- Match partner's language (Ukrainian for UA, Russian for RU, etc.)
- Simple, clear language — no jargon without explanation
- Numbers in both $ and local currency when applicable

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] IMMEDIATE NEXT ACTION block at the very top?
- [ ] Honest P(failure) disclosed with source?
- [ ] Revenue from sources, not invented?
- [ ] Risk section present with all 5 risks?
- [ ] Tax options for partner's country?
- [ ] Profit split model clearly stated with example?
- [ ] Partner provides ≤5 things — nothing more?
- [ ] Beautiful HTML (not plain markdown)?
- [ ] Table of contents with anchors?
- [ ] CTA with single next action?

## Pitfalls

- ❌ **Optimistic revenue**: Default is to be too optimistic. Always disclose "these are NOT guarantees."
- ❌ **Missing risk disclosure**: If the plan doesn't mention P(failure), it's incomplete. Fix it.
- ❌ **Tax avoidance**: Never suggest tax evasion. Always recommend official registration.
- ❌ **No CTA**: Every business plan must end with "what to do right now." Without it, it's just information.
- ❌ **Partner overload**: More than 5 partner tasks = partner won't do it. Keep it minimal.
- ❌ **Hidden fees**: Reveal ALL costs upfront (proxy, account purchase, tools).
