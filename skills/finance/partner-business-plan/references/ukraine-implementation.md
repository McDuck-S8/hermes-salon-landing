## Legal Warnings

- **Tax evasion is a criminal offense in Ukraine.** Always recommend official tax registration.
- **P2P limits may be reinstated.** НБУ monitors crypto flows — diversify across 2-3 cards.
- **Bank account freezing is possible** if flagged for suspicious activity. Maintain paper trail: contracts, invoices, CPA reports.
- **VPN is legal** in Ukraine. No geo-restrictions on crypto exchanges.
