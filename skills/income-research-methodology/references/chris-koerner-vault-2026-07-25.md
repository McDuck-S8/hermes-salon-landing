## Presentation Format Analysis

### Title Structure
"{Strong adjective} {Business type} {Result/concept}" 
Examples:
- "The Simplest Side Hustle You Can Start Under $100"
- "Forget Dropshipping. This Side Hustle Is 10x Easier (and Smarter)"
- "The Most Hands-Off $192K/M Business Anyone Can Copy"

Pattern: [Benefit hook] + [Specific constraint] + [Result]
NEVER: "How to make money" (too generic)

### Description Structure
1. **Immediate business plan CTA** — "We put together a step-by-step business plan for X"
2. **Specific link to Stripe payment** — "buy.stripe.com/..." ($19, promo code)
3. **Links to free assets** — newsletter, community, social
4. **Tags** — 30+ hashtags covering: business type, income model, tool, outcome

### Video Structure
- No intro fluff — straight into the business model
- Real demonstration (AI building a site, showing numbers)
- Anchor numbers ("$192K/month", "under $100", "4,967 views")
- Money model explained explicitly (not implied)
- CTA at end: business plan, newsletter, community

### Funnel / Monetization
1. Free video (YouTube) → 
2. Business plan ($19, one-time) → 
3. Newsletter (free, weekly) → 
4. Community ($, recurring, TKOwners.com) → 
5. Merch (T-shirts, $29-32)
