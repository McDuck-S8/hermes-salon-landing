# Chris Koerner (The Koerner Office) — Format Analysis

## Channel Overview

**Channel:** The Koerner Office (@thekoerneroffice)  
**Content type:** Side hustle / business opportunity case studies  
**Format pattern:** Every video = one complete scheme with exact numbers, mechanism, and a paid business plan

## Title Formula

`[Hook] + [Result/Claim] + [Barrier]`

Examples:
- "The Simplest Side Hustle You Can Start Under $100" (hook + result + barrier=$100)
- "These 'Boring' Items Make Him $17.3k/Month (Side Hustle)" (hook + specific result)
- "The Most Hands-Off $192K/M Business Anyone Can Copy" (claim + barrier=anyone)
- "The Side Hustle That Pays My Mortgage, No Social Media Required!" (result + barrier=no social media)

Pattern: **Always a specific number or barrier.** Never vague "make money online" titles. Every title answers "what, how much, how hard?"

## Description Structure (Critical — This IS the Scheme)

Koerner's video descriptions ARE the business plan. Every description follows this exact structure:

1. **Lead with paid offer** (first sentence = business plan link with discount code)
2. **Case study intro** (numbers first: "Josh turned $90 into $50K in a year")
3. **Exact mechanism** (how it works in 1-2 sentences: "finds local businesses in Facebook groups, closes 95% over text")
4. **Specific claim** (no generalizations: "one plumber paid him $1,100 in five messages")
5. **Resource links** (bookmarks, tools, related content)
6. **Funnel** (free video → $19 business plan → $5/mo membership → $25/mo community)

## Value Creation Funnel (Two-Level)

### Level 1: The Featured Business
The person Koerner interviews:
- Information: knows a market need (businesses need customers)
- Asset: builds a service (postcard mail, directory, lead gen)
- Income: side effect of connecting supply to demand ($4-5K/run, $50K/year)

### Level 2: Koerner's Meta-Business
Koerner himself:
- Information: finds Level 1 case studies
- Asset: creates business plans ($19 each), newsletter (free → paid), membership ($5/mo), community ($25/mo)
- Income: side effect of helping his audience launch Level 1 businesses

**Both levels follow the same law: create value → build asset → income as side effect.**

## Facet Analysis

Every Koerner video is NOT a "new topic" — it's a **facet of one model**:

Connected Facets:
| Video | Facet | Value Delivery Mechanism |
|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| Directory Site | AI-powered catalog | Listings + leads for local businesses |
| Lead Gen | SEO capture | Recurring $500-2000/mo lead sales |
| Postcard | Physical mail | B2B direct mail service |
| Credit Card | Info arbitrage | Newsletter + consulting |
| Boring Items | Product resale | Low-competition niche flipping |

One model: **Connect someone who needs customers (business) with someone who needs services (consumer).**

## Presentation Takeaways for Hermes

When building assets from YouTube findings:

1. **Lead with the offer, not the explanation.** Koerner's first line is always what you get.
2. **Use specific numbers.** "$90 → $50K" works. "Make good money" doesn't.
3. **Structure over three levels:**
   - Level 0: Raw information (the YouTube video)
   - Level 1: Extracted scheme (platform → action → value → income as side effect)
   - Level 2: Packaged asset (blueprint, guide, article, or tool that helps others replicate Level 1)
4. **Every asset needs a funnel.** Free content → low-ticket product → subscription → community.
5. **Study the creator, not just the content.** Why does Koerner use $19, not $9 or $29? Why does he lead with the business plan link before the description? These choices are the real lesson.
