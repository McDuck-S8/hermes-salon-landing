# Telegram CPA & Affiliate Marketing (2026)

## Key Findings

### Telegram Affiliate Program (Native)
- Earn Stars from mini app referrals
- Browse programs in Settings > My Stars
- Free to join
- Commission on Star purchases by referred users

### Telegram CPA Funnel
- Bot replaces landing page
- Traffic from Meta/YouTube/push → bot introduces offer → CTA → upsell
- Bot logic = email + retargeting + CRM combined
- Early phase 2026 = cheap traffic

### Telegram Ads
- CPM from $0.015/click
- GEO + channel interest targeting
- Frequency capping = built-in retargeting
- Still underused and underpriced

### Tara Bot Model (57★ GitHub)
- Telegram bot that searches flights (Google Flights)
- Compares prices (Google Shopping)
- Injects affiliate links
- Daily price monitoring
- Uses Claude AI
- Proven model for affiliate monetization
- Repo: https://github.com/thaolst/tara-bot

### MyLead Platform
- Free publisher account
- Route funnels to high-paying campaigns
- Test with minimal budget

## Monetization Patterns
1. **Bot as funnel**: User asks → bot searches → compares → affiliate link → commission
2. **Channel + bot**: Content channel drives traffic to bot → bot converts
3. **Daily monitoring**: Price alerts → affiliate links → recurring commissions

## Low-Cost Entry
- Build cost: $300-600
- Revenue: $5-50/member/month
- Subscription: $5-15/month
- Affiliate: 3-10% commission
